# Privacy Policy for Mnemoflux

**Last updated: October 7, 2026**

Mnemoflux ("we", "our", or "the app") is a learning and memory application that helps users create and study flashcards, including AI-assisted mnemonic content and images.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, and the choices available to users when using Mnemoflux.

## 1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Mnemoflux, we may process:

- Account information, such as your email address and account identifier.
- Content you create in the app, including decks, flashcards, mnemonic stories, and related learning content.
- Learning and progress information, such as study activity, progress, and usage of app features.
- Subscription and entitlement information required to determine whether an account has access to paid features.
- Advertising-related information and device identifiers where permitted by applicable law and your consent choices.
- Technical information necessary to operate, secure, diagnose, and improve the app.

We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information as part of the normal use of Mnemoflux.

## 2. AI Features

Mnemoflux provides AI-assisted features, including generation of mnemonic content and images.

When you request an AI feature, relevant content from your flashcard or mnemonic may be processed by external AI service providers in order to generate the requested result.

Users should not submit sensitive personal information, confidential information, or information they do not have permission to process through AI features.

## 3. Advertising

The free version of Mnemoflux may display advertisements using Google AdMob.

Advertising may include rewarded advertisements that users choose to watch in exchange for access to certain AI generation actions, as well as interstitial advertisements at appropriate points in the app.

Google and its advertising partners may process device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, interaction data, and other information for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, and related purposes, subject to applicable law and the user's consent choices.

For users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, Mnemoflux uses Google's consent management mechanisms to request applicable privacy choices before serving advertising where required.

## 4. Subscriptions and Payments

Mnemoflux may offer paid subscriptions through Google Play. Subscription entitlement management is provided using RevenueCat.

Mnemoflux does not directly receive or store your full payment card information. Payments are processed by the applicable app store/payment provider.

RevenueCat may process purchase identifiers, subscription status, app user identifiers, and related transaction information required to provide subscription functionality.

## 5. Backend and Account Services

Mnemoflux uses Supabase for backend functionality such as authentication, database storage, and server-side application functions.

Information associated with your account and app usage may therefore be processed and stored using Supabase infrastructure.

## 6. How We Use Information

We process information as necessary to:

- provide and operate Mnemoflux;
- authenticate users and maintain accounts;
- save decks, flashcards, progress, and other app content;
- provide AI-assisted mnemonic features;
- provide and verify subscription access;
- provide advertising and rewarded-ad functionality;
- maintain security and prevent abuse or fraud;
- diagnose technical problems and improve the reliability of the app;
- comply with applicable legal obligations.

## 7. Third-Party Services

Mnemoflux currently uses or may interact with services provided by third parties, including:

- Google, including Google Play and Google AdMob;
- Supabase;
- RevenueCat;
- AI service providers used to generate AI-assisted content and images.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable data protection agreements.

## 8. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Mnemoflux, maintain an active account, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud and abuse, and enforce applicable agreements.

Information may be deleted or anonymized when it is no longer required, subject to legal and operational retention requirements.

## 9. Account and Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their Mnemoflux account and associated personal data.

Instructions for requesting account deletion will be made available through Mnemoflux and/or an associated public account-deletion page.

Some information may be retained where required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, accounting, or regulatory purposes.

## 10. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing and, where applicable, withdraw consent.

Advertising consent choices can be managed through the privacy options provided in the app where required.

## 11. Children's Privacy

Mnemoflux is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

If we become aware that personal information from a child has been collected in circumstances where parental consent or another legal basis was required but not obtained, we will take appropriate steps to delete or otherwise address that information.

## 12. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information processed through Mnemoflux.

However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 13. International Processing

Service providers used by Mnemoflux may process information in countries other than the country in which the user resides.

Where required, appropriate safeguards are used for international transfers of personal information.

## 14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Mnemoflux, its service providers, or applicable legal requirements change.

The current version will be made available at this public Privacy Policy URL, with the latest revision date shown above.
